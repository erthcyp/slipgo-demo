FOR DEMONSTRATING THE SLIPGO FLOW · NOTHING HERE IS REAL
HOW TO USE THESE These are made up. No shop issued them, no car was parked and no money changed hands. They exist so the flow can be shown without buying anything first.
  1. Scan the QR on the parking ticket below. It opens the SlipGo demo, the same way a real ticket QR opens the mall's payment page
  2. The demo shows the parking session and the fee due
  3. Upload or photograph one of the slips below. Slips from joined shops carry a SlipGo code, so they clear instantly. Slips without one go to OUR DESK to be checked by hand
  4. Watch the fee drop to zero once the ฿500 threshold is reached
  5. Photo receipts sit in OUR DESK until someone approves them by hand — that manual step is the whole point of Tier 3
